The Devil’s Power to Deceive

The devil has great power to deceive;6 he will so transform things as to make one gape at those who are doing the will of God.7 You need not be teasing your husbands because of their deeds, but let the weight of your innocence, kindness and affection be felt, which is more mighty than a millstone hung about the neck;8 not war, not jangle, not contradiction, or dispute, but meekness, love, purity—these are the things that should magnify you in the eyes of all good men. Achan [see Joshua 7]9 must be brought to light, iniquity must be purged10 out from the midst of the Saints; then the veil will be rent,11 and the blessings of heaven12 will flow down—they will roll down like the Mississippi river.13

If this Society listen to the counsel14 of the Almighty, through the heads of the Church,15 they shall have power to command queens in their midst.16

I now deliver it as a prophecy, if the inhabitants of this state, with the people of the surrounding country, will turn unto the Lord with all their hearts,17 ten years will not roll around before the kings and queens of the earth will come unto Zion,18 and pay their respects to the leaders of this people; they shall come with their millions, and shall contribute of their abundance for the relief of the poor, and the building up and beautifying of Zion.

After this instruction, you will be responsible for your own sins;19 it is a desirable honor that you should so walk before our heavenly Father20 as to save yourselves;21 we are all responsible to God for the manner we improve the light22 and wisdom given by our Lord to enable us to save ourselves.

Beware of Self-Righteousness

President Smith continued reading from the above-mentioned chapter,23 and to give instructions respecting the order of God,24 as established in the Church, saying everyone should aspire only to magnify his own office and calling.25
