STPJS 309

Many men will say, “I will never forsake you,1 but will stand by you2 at all times.” But the moment you teach them3 some of the mysteries of the kingdom of God4 that are retained in the heavens5 and are to be revealed to the children of men6 when they are prepared for them they will be the first to stone you7 and put you to death.8 It was this same principle that crucified the Lord Jesus Christ, and will cause the people to kill the prophets in this generation.

Many things are insoluble to the children of men9 in the last days: for instance, that God should raise the dead,10 and forgetting that things have been hid from before the foundation of the world,11 which are to be revealed to babes in the last days.12

Some Men “Too Wise to Be Taught”

There are a great many wise men and women too in our midst who are too wise to be taught;13 therefore they must die in their ignorance,14 and in the resurrection they will find their mistake.15 Many seal up the door of heaven16 by saying, So far God may reveal and I will believe.

All men who become heirs of God and joint heirs with Jesus Christ17 will have to receive the fulness of the ordinances18 of his kingdom; and those who will not receive all the ordinances19 will come short20 of the fullness of that glory,21 if they do not lose the whole.

Paradise

I will say something about the spirits in prison.22 There has been much said by modern divines about the words of Jesus (when on the cross) to the thief, saying, “This day shalt thou be with me in paradise.”23 King James’ translators make it out to say paradise. But what is paradise? It is a modern word: it does not answer at all to the original word that Jesus made use of. Find the original of the word paradise. You may as easily find a needle in a haymow. Here is a chance for battle, ye learned men. There is nothing in the original word in Greek from which this was taken that signifies paradise; but it was—This day thou shalt be with me in the world of spirits:24 then I will teach you all about it and answer your inquiries. And Peter says he went and preached to the world of spirits (spirits in prison, 1 Peter, 3rd chap. 19th verse),25 so that they who would receive it could have it answered by proxy by those who live on the earth,26 etc.
