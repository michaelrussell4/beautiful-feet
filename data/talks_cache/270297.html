Salvation Through Knowledge

It is not wisdom that we should have all knowledge at once presented before us;1 but that we should have a little at a time;2 then we can comprehend it. President Smith then read the 2nd Epistle of Peter, 1st chapter, 16th to last verses,3 and dwelt upon the 19th verse4 with some remarks.

Add to your faith knowledge,5 etc. The principle of knowledge is the principle of salvation.6 This principle can be comprehended by the faithful and diligent;7 and every one that does not obtain knowledge sufficient to be saved will be condemned.8 The principle of salvation is given us through the knowledge9 of Jesus Christ.10

Salvation Is to Triumph over Enemies

Salvation11 is nothing more nor less than to triumph over all12 our enemies13 and put them under our feet. And when we have power to put all enemies under our feet14 in this world, and a knowledge to triumph over all evil spirits15 in the world to come, then we are saved, as in the case of Jesus, who was to reign until He had put all enemies under His feet,16 and the last enemy was death.17

No Salvation Without a Tabernacle

Perhaps there are principles here that few men have thought of. No person can have this salvation except through a tabernacle.

Now, in this world, mankind are naturally selfish,18 ambitious19 and striving to excel one above another;20 yet some are willing to build up others as well as themselves.21 So in the other world there are a variety of spirits. Some seek to excel.22 And this was the case with Lucifer when he fell.23 He sought for things which were unlawful.24 Hence he was sent down,25 and it is said he drew many away with him;26 and the greatness of his punishment is that he shall not have a tabernacle.27 This is his punishment.
