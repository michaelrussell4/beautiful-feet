Gospel Always the Same

Now taking it for granted that the scriptures say what they mean, and mean what they say, we have sufficient grounds to go on and prove from the Bible that the gospel has always been the same;4 the ordinances to fulfill its requirements, the same, and the officers to officiate, the same;5 and the signs and fruits resulting from the promises, the same: therefore, as Noah was a preacher of righteousness6 he must have been baptized and ordained to the priesthood by the laying on of the hands,7 etc. For no man taketh this honor unto himself except he be called of God as was Aaron,8 and Aaron was baptized in the cloud and in the sea, together with all Israel, as is related by the Apostle in Corinthians.9 This position or fact, is witnessed in this manner: the covenant of circumcision made with Abraham,10 and practiced steadily up to the departing of Israel out of Egypt, was abandoned in the wilderness, forty years—and renewed by Joshua after he passed over Jordan, and encamped at Gilgal, where he made sharp knives and circumcised the whole male portion of the church.11

Man Must Be Born Again

Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again he cannot see the kingdom of God.
