STPJS 266

Enoch was righteous enough to come into the presence of God, and walk with him,1 he must have become so by keeping his commandments,2 and so of every righteous person,3 whether it was Noah, a preacher of righteousness;4 Abraham, the father of the faithful;5 Jacob, the prevailer with God;6 Moses, the man who wrote of Christ,7 and brought forth the law by commandment,8 as a schoolmaster to bring men to Christ,9 or whether it was Jesus Christ himself, who had no need of repentance,10 having no sin,11 according to his solemn declaration to John:—now let me be baptized:12 for no man can enter the kingdom without obeying this ordinance:13 for thus it becometh us to fulfil ALL RIGHTEOUSNESS.14 Surely, then, if it became John and Jesus Christ, the Savior, to fulfil all righteousness to be baptized—so surely, then, it will become every other person that seeks the kingdom of heaven to go and do likewise;15 for he is the door, and if any person climbs up any other way, the same is a thief and a robber!16

Baptism Required in All Ages

In the former ages of the world, before the Savior came in the flesh,17 “the saints”18 were baptized19 in the name of Jesus Christ to come,20 because there never was any other name whereby men could be saved;21 and after he came in the flesh and was crucified, then the saints were baptized in the name of Jesus Christ,22 crucified, risen from the dead23 and ascended into heaven,24 that they might be buried in baptism like him,25 and be raised in glory like him,26 that as there was but one Lord, one faith, one baptism,27 and one God and father of us all,28 even so there was but one door29 to the mansions of bliss.30 Amen. (Sept. 1, 1842.) T&S 3:902–905.
