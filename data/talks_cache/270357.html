Hence God hath made a provision that every spirit in the eternal world can be ferreted out and saved unless he has committed that unpardonable sin which cannot be remitted to him either in this world1 or the world of spirits.2 God has wrought out a salvation3 for all men,4 unless they have committed a certain sin; and every man who has a friend in the eternal world can save him, unless he has committed the unpardonable sin.5 And so you can see how far you can be a savior.6

The Unpardonable Sin

A man cannot commit the unpardonable sin7 after the dissolution of the body, and there is a way possible for escape. Knowledge saves8 a man; and in the world of spirits9 no man can be exalted10 but by knowledge. So long as a man will not give heed to the commandments,11 he must abide without salvation. If a man has knowledge, he can be saved;12 although, if he has been guilty of great sins, he will be punished for them.13 But when he consents to obey the Gospel, whether here or in the world of spirits, he is saved.14

A man is his own tormenter15 and his own condemner.16 Hence the saying, They shall go into the lake that burns with fire and brimstone.17 The torment of disappointment18 in the mind of man is as exquisite19 as a lake burning with fire and brimstone.20 I say, so is the torment of man.

I know the Scriptures21 and understand them. I said, no man can commit the unpardonable sin22 after the dissolution of the body, nor in this life, until he receives the Holy Ghost;23 but they must do it in this world. Hence the salvation of Jesus Christ was wrought out for all men,24 in order to triumph over the devil;25 for if it did not catch him in one place, it would in another; for he stood up as a Savior.26 All will suffer27 until they obey Christ himself.28

The contention in heaven was—Jesus said there would be certain souls that would not be saved;29 and the devil said he could save them all,30 and laid his plans before the grand council,31 who gave their vote in favor of Jesus Christ.32 So the devil rose up in rebellion against God,33 and was cast down,34 with all who put up their heads for him.35 (Book of Moses—Pearl of Great Price, Chap. 4:1–4;36 Book of Abraham, Chap. 3:23–28.)37
