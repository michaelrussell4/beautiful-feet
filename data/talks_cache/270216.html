Some have supposed that Brother Joseph could not die; but this is a mistake: it is true there have been times when I have had the promise of my life2 to accomplish such and such things, but, having now accomplished those things, I have not at present any lease of my life, I am as liable to die as other men.

I can say in my heart, that I have not done anything against Ephraim Marks that I am sorry for, and I would ask any of his companions if they have anything against him that they are sorry for, or that they would not like to meet and answer for at the bar of God,3 if so, let it prove as a warning to all to deal justly4 before God, and with all mankind, then we shall be clear in the day of judgment.5

When we lose a near and dear friend, upon whom we have set our hearts,6 it should be a caution unto us not to set our affections7 too firmly upon others, knowing that they in like manner be taken from us. Our affections should be placed upon God8 and His work, more intensely than upon our fellow beings. (April 9, 1842.) DHC 4:587.

Synopsis of Remarks of the Prophet—Reproof of all Wickedness

Joseph the Seer9 arose in the power of God;10 reproved and rebuked wickedness before the people, in the name of the Lord God. He wished to say a few words to suit the condition of the general mass, and then said: I shall speak with authority of the Priesthood11 in the name of the Lord God,12 which shall prove a savor13 of life unto life, or of death unto death.14 Notwithstanding this congregation profess to be Saints,15 yet I stand in the midst of all [kinds of] characters and classes of men. If you wish to go where God is,16 you must be like God,17 or possess the principles which God possesses,18 for if we are not drawing towards God19 in principle, we are going from Him and drawing towards the devil. Yes, I am standing in the midst of all kinds of people.

Search your hearts,20 and see if you are like God.21 I have searched mine, and feel to repent of all my sins.
