STPJS 358
The Forgiveness of Sins

All sins shall be forgiven, except the sin against the Holy Ghost;1 for Jesus will save all except the sons of perdition.2 What must a man do to commit the unpardonable sin?3 He must receive the Holy Ghost, have the heavens opened unto him,4 and know God,5 and then sin against Him. After a man has sinned against the Holy Ghost, there is no repentance for him.6,a He has got to say that the sun does not shine while he sees it; he has got to deny Jesus Christ7 when the heavens have been opened unto him,8 and to deny the plan of salvation9 with his eyes open to the truth of it; and from that time he begins to be an enemy. This is the case with many apostates of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.10

When a man begins to be an enemy to this work, he hunts me, he seeks to kill me, and never ceases to thirst for my blood.11 He gets the spirit of the devil12—the same spirit that they had who crucified the Lord of Life—the same spirit that sins against the Holy Ghost.13 You cannot save such persons;14 you cannot bring them to repentance; they make open war, like the devil,15 and awful is the consequence.16

I advise all of you to be careful what you do, or you may by-and-by find out that you have been deceived.17 Stay yourselves;18 do not give way;19 don’t make any hasty moves, you may be saved. If a spirit of bitterness20 is in you, don’t be in haste.21 You may say, that man is a sinner.22 Well, if he repents, he shall be forgiven.23 Be cautious:24 await. When you find a spirit that wants bloodshed25—murder, the same is not of God,26 but is of the devil.27 Out of the abundance of the heart of man the mouth speaketh.28

“In My Father’s House”

The best men bring forth the best works.29 The man who tells you words of life30 is the man who can save you. I warn you against all evil characters who sin against the Holy Ghost;31
