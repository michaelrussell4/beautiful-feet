I could go back and trace every subject of interest concerning the relationship of man to God, if I had time. I can enter into the mysteries; I can enter largely into the eternal worlds;3 for Jesus said, “In my Father’s house are many mansions; if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you” (John 14:2).4 Paul says, “There is one glory of the sun, and another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars; for one star differeth from another star in glory. So also is the resurrection of the dead” (1 Cor. 15:41).5 What have we to console us in relation to the dead?6 We have reason to have the greatest hope and consolations for our dead of any people on the earth; for we have seen them walk worthily7 in our midst, and seen them sink asleep in the arms of Jesus;8 and those who have died in the faith9 are now in the celestial kingdom of God.10 And hence is the glory of the sun.11

Righteous Mourners Rejoice

You mourners have occasion to rejoice, speaking of the death of Elder King Follett; for your husband and father is gone to wait until the resurrection of the dead12—until the perfection of the remainder; for at the resurrection your friend will rise in perfect felicity13 and go to celestial glory,14 while many must wait myriads of years before they can receive the like blessings; and your expectations and hopes are far above what man can conceive;15 for why has God revealed it to us?

I am authorized to say, by the authority of the Holy Ghost,16 that you have no occasion to fear; for he is gone to the home of the just.17 Don’t mourn, don’t weep.18 I know it by the testimony of the Holy Ghost19 that is within me; and you may wait for your friends to come forth to meet you in the morn of the celestial world.20

Rejoice, O Israel!21 Your friends who have been murdered for the truth’s sake22 in the persecutions23 shall triumph gloriously24 in the celestial world, while their murderers shall welter25 for ages in torment, even until they shall have paid the uttermost farthing.26 I say this for the benefit of strangers.
