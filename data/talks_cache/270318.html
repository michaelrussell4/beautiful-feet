Excerpts from a Sermon by President Joseph Smith

July 23rd, 1843.

Last Monday morning certain men came to me and said: “Brother Joseph, Hyrum is no prophet1—he can’t lead the church; you must lead the church. If you resign, all things will go wrong; you must not resign; if you do the church will be scattered.” I felt curious and said: “Have we not learned the Priesthood after the order of Melchizedek,2 which includes both Prophets, Priests and Kings: see Rev. 1 Chap., 6th v.,3 and I will advance your Prophet to a Priest, and then to a King—not to the Kingdoms of this earth, but of the Most High God.4 See Rev. 5 Chap., 10th v.—‘Thou hast made us unto our God, Kings and Priests, and we shall reign on the earth.’”5

If I should be exalted would there not be a great many of my enemies disappointed in Missouri, when they wake up and find themselves in hell, see what they might have obtained,6 and realize what they have lost by not listening to my voice,7 and obeying my instructions?8

Matth. 5 Chap., 17 and 18 v.—“Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets; I am not come to destroy but to fulfil. For verily I say unto you, till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law till all be fulfilled.”9

And again, Matth. 11 Chap., 12 and 13 v.—“And from the days of John the Baptist, until now, the Kingdom of Heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force. For all the prophets and the law prophesied until John.”10 John held the Aaronic Priesthood11 and was a legal administrator, and the forerunner of Christ, and came to prepare the way before him.12

Christ was the head of the Church,13 the chief corner stone,14 the spiritual rock15 upon which the church was built, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.16 He built up the Kingdom, chose Apostles,17 and ordained them to the Melchizedek Priesthood,18 giving them power to administer in the ordinances of the Gospel.19 John was a priest after the order of Aaron20 before Christ.

See Exodus 30 Chap. 30 and 31 v.—“And thou shalt anoint Aaron and his sons, and consecrate them, that they may minister unto me in the priest’s office. And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying, This shall be an holy anointing
