STPJS 361

There is one God, one Father, one Jesus, one hope of our calling, one baptism.1 * * * Many talk of baptism not being essential to salvation; but this kind of teaching would lay the foundation of their damnation.2 I have the truth, and am at the defiance of the world to contradict me, if they can.

I have now preached a little Latin, a little Hebrew, Greek, and German; and I have fulfilled all. I am not so big a fool as many have taken me to be. The Germans know that I read the German correctly.

A Call to Repentance

Hear it, all ye ends of the earth3—all ye priests, all ye sinners, and all men. Repent! repent! Obey the Gospel. Turn to God;4 for your religion won’t save you, and you will be damned. I do not say how long. There have been remarks made concerning all men being redeemed5 from hell; but I say that those who sin against the Holy Ghost cannot be forgiven in this world or in the world to come;6 they shall die the second death.7 Those who commit the unpardonable sin8 are doomed to Gnolom9—to dwell in hell, worlds without end.10 As they concoct scenes of bloodshed in this world, so they shall rise to that resurrection which is as the lake of fire and brimstone.11 Some shall rise to the everlasting burnings of God;12 for God dwells in everlasting burnings,13 and some shall rise to the damnation of their own filthiness,14 which is as exquisite a torment15 as the lake of fire and brimstone.

I have intended my remarks for all, both rich and poor, bond and free,16 great and small. I have no enmity17 against any man. I love you all;18 but I hate some of your deeds.19 I am your best friend, and if persons miss their mark it is their own fault. If I reprove a man, and he hates me,20 he is a fool; for I love all men, especially these my brethren and sisters.21

I rejoice in hearing the testimony of my aged friends. You don’t know me; you never knew my heart.22 No man knows my history. I cannot tell it: I shall never undertake it. I don’t blame any one for not believing my history. If I had not experienced what I have, I could not have believed it myself. I never did harm any man23 since I was born in the world. My voice is always for peace.24
