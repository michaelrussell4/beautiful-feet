The Saints Subject to Divine Counsel

In regard to the building up of Zion,5 it has to be done by the counsel of Jehovah, by the revelations of heaven; and we should feel to say, “If the Lord go not with us, carry us not up hence.”6 We would say to the Saints that come here, we have laid the foundation for the gathering of God’s people7 to this place, and they expect that when the Saints do come, they will be under the counsel that God has appointed.8 The Twelve are set apart to counsel the Saints pertaining to this matter; and we expect that those who come here will send before them their wise men9 according to revelation; or if not practicable, be subject to the counsel that God has given,10 or they cannot receive an inheritance11 among the Saints, or be considered as God’s people,12 and they will be dealt with as transgressors13 of the laws of God.14 We are trying here to gird up our loins,15 and purge from our midst the workers of iniquity;16 and we hope that when our brethren arrive from abroad, they will assist us to roll forth this good work,17 and to accomplish this great design that “Zion may be built up in righteousness;18 and all nations flock to her standard;”19 that as God’s people, under His direction, and obedient to His law,20 we may grow up in righteousness and truth;21 that when His purposes shall be accomplished,22 we may receive an inheritance among those that are sanctified.23 (July 15, 1842.) DHC 5:61–66.

Letter of the Prophet to Governor Carlin —
Satisfied with the Governor’s Attitude

Esteemed Sir:—Your favor of the 27th instant per Brevet Major-General Wilson Law is before me. I cannot let this opportunity pass without tendering to you my warmest thanks for the friendly treatment my lady as well as those with her received at your hands during the late visit, and also for the friendly feelings breathed forth in your letter. Your Excellency
