There is another error which opens a door for the adversary to enter.4 As females possess refined feelings and sensitiveness, they are also subject to overmuch zeal,5 which must ever prove dangerous, and cause them to be rigid in a religious capacity—[they] should be armed with mercy,6 notwithstanding the iniquity among us.7

The Spirit of Forgiveness

Said he had been instrumental in bringing iniquity to light8—it was a melancholy thought and awful that so many should place themselves under the condemnation of the devil,9 and going to perdition.10 With deep feeling he said that they are fellow mortals, we loved them once, shall we not encourage them to reformation? We have not yet forgiven them seventy times seven,11 as our Savior directed; perhaps we have not forgiven them once. There is now a day of salvation12 to such as repent and reform;—and they who repent not should be cast out13 from this society; yet we should woo them to return14 to God, lest they escape not the damnation of hell!15 Where there is a mountain top, there is also a valley—we should act in all things on a proper medium to every immortal spirit.16 Notwithstanding the unworthy are among us,17 the virtuous should not, from self-importance, grieve and oppress needlessly, those unfortunate ones—even these should be encouraged to hereafter live to be honored18 by this society, who are the best portions of the community. Said he had two things to recommend to the members of this society, to put a double watch over the tongue;19 no organized body can exist without this at all. All organized bodies have their peculiar evils, weaknesses20 and difficulties, the object is to make those not so good reform and return to the path of virtue that they may be numbered with the good,21 and even hold the keys of power,22 which will influence to virtue and goodness—should chasten and reprove,
