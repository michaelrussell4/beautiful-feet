Stakes of Zion

I have received instructions from the Lord9 that from henceforth wherever Elders of Israel10 shall build up churches11 and branches unto the Lord throughout the States, there shall be a stake of Zion.12 In the great cities, as Boston,13 New York,14 &c., there shall be stakes. It is a glorious proclamation, and I reserved it to the last, and designed it to be understood that this work shall commence after the washings, anointings, and endowments15 have been performed here.

The Lord has an established law in relation to the matter: there must be a particular spot for the salvation of our dead. I verily believe there will be a place, and hence men who want to save their dead can come and bring their families, do their work by being baptized and attending to the other ordinances for their dead,16 and then may go back again to live and wait till they go to receive their reward.17 I shall leave my brethren to enlarge on this subject; it is my duty to teach the doctrine.18 I would teach it more fully19—the spirit is willing but the flesh is weak.20 God is not willing to let me gratify you; but I must teach the Elders, and they should teach you.21 God made Aaron to be the mouthpiece for the children of Israel,22 and He will make me be god to you23 in His stead,24 and the Elders to be mouth for me; and if you don’t like it, you must lump it. I have been giving Elder Adams instruction in some principles to speak to you, and if he makes a mistake, I will get up and correct him. (April 8, 1844.) DHC 6:318–320.
