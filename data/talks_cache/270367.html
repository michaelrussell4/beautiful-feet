The Resurrection Universal

In regard to the law of the Priesthood,1 there should be a place where all nations shall come up2 from time to time to receive their endowments;3 and the Lord has said this shall be the place for the baptisms for the dead.4 Every man that has been baptized and belongs to the kingdom has a right to be baptized for those who have gone before; and as soon as the law of the Gospel5 is obeyed here by their friends who act as proxy for them, the Lord has administrators there to set them free.6 A man may act as proxy for his own relatives; the ordinances of the Gospel which were laid out before the foundations of the world7 have thus been fulfilled by them, and we may be baptized for those whom we have much friendship for; but it must first be revealed to the man of God,8 lest we should run too far.9 “As in Adam all die, even so in Christ shall all be made alive;”10 all shall be raised from the dead.11 The Lamb of God hath brought to pass the resurrection,12 so that all shall rise from the dead.13

God Almighty Himself dwells in eternal fire;14 flesh and blood cannot go there,15 for all corruption is devoured by the fire. “Our God is a consuming fire.”16 When our flesh is quickened by the Spirit,17 there will be no blood in this tabernacle. Some dwell in higher glory than others.18

Those who have done wrong always have that wrong gnawing them.19 Immortality dwells in everlasting burnings.20 I will from time to time reveal to you the subjects that are revealed by the Holy Ghost to me.21 All the lies22 that are now hatched up23 against me are of the devil,24 and the influence of the devil and his servants will be used against the kingdom of God.25 The servants of God26 teach nothing but principles of eternal life, by their works ye shall know them.27 A good man will speak good things and holy principles, and an evil man evil things.28 I feel, in the name of the Lord, to rebuke all such bad principles, liars,29 &c., and I warn all of you to look out whom you are going after. I exhort you to give heed to all the virtue and the teachings30 which I have given you.
