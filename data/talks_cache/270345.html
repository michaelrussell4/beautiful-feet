STPJS 345

I have the truth of God,1 and show that ninety-nine out of every hundred professing religious ministers are false teachers,2 having no authority,3 while they pretend to hold the keys of God’s kingdom4 on earth, and was to kill them because they are false teachers,5 it would deluge the whole world with blood.

I will prove that the world is wrong, by showing what God is. I am going to enquire after God;6 for I want you all to know him, and to be familiar with him;7 and if I am bringing you to a knowledge of him,8 all persecutions against me ought to cease. You will then know that I am his servant;9 for I speak as one having authority.10

God an Exalted Man

I will go back to the beginning before the world was,11 to show what kind of being God is. What sort of a being was God in the beginning? Open your ears and hear,12 all ye ends of the earth,13 for I am going to prove it to you by the Bible, and to tell you the designs of God14 in relation to the human race, and why He interferes with the affairs of man.

God himself was once as we are now, and is an exalted man, and sits enthroned in yonder heavens! That is the great secret. If the veil were rent15 today, and the great God who holds this world in its orbit, and who upholds all worlds16 and all things by his power, was to make himself visible,—I say, if you were to see him today, you would see him like a man in form17—like yourselves in all the person, image, and very form as a man;18 for Adam was created in the very fashion, image and likeness of God,19 and received instruction from, and walked, talked and conversed with him,20 as one man talks and communes with another.21

In order to understand the subject of the dead, for consolation of those who mourn for the loss of their friends, it is necessary we should understand the character and being of God and how he came to be so; for I am going to tell you how God came to be God. We have imagined and supposed that God was God from all eternity.22 I will refute that idea, and take away the veil, so that you may see.
