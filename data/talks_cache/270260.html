Baptism for the Dead

A few very important things have been manifested to me in my absence respecting the doctrine of baptism for the dead, which I shall communicate to the Saints next Sabbath, if nothing should occur to prevent me.

* * *

President Smith said, “I have one remark to make respecting the baptism for the dead1 to suffice for the time being, until I have opportunity to discuss the subject at greater length—all persons baptized for the dead must have a recorder present,2 that he may be an eyewitness to record3 and testify of the truth4 and validity of his record. It will be necessary, in the Grand Council, that these things be testified to by competent witnesses. Therefore let the recording and witnessing of baptisms for the dead be carefully attended to from this time forth. If there is any lack, it may be at the expense of our friends; they may not come forth.”5 (Aug. 31, 1842.) DHC 5:139–141.

Persecution the Heritage of the Righteous
From an Editorial in the “Times and Seasons,” by the Prophet

* * *

Abel was slain for his righteousness,6 and how many more up to the flood is not of much consequence to us now. But if we believe in present revelation, as published in the “Times and Seasons” last spring, Abraham, the prophet of the Lord, was laid upon the iron bedstead for slaughter;7 and the book of Jasher,8 which has not been disproved as a bad author, says he was cast into the fire of the Chaldees. Moses, the man of God,9 who killed an Egyptian persecutor of the children of Israel, was driven from his country and kindred.10 Elijah had to flee his country, for they sought his life,11—and he was fed by ravens.12 Daniel was cast into a den of lions:13 Micaiah was fed on the bread of affliction;14 and Jeremiah was cast into the filthy hole under the Temple;15 and did these afflictions come upon these prophets of the Lord on account of transgression? No! It was the iron hand of persecution16—like the chains of Missouri! And mark—when these old prophets suffered, the vengeance of God, in due time,17 followed and left the wicked opposers of the Lord’s anointed18 like Sodom and Gomorrah;19 like the Egyptians;20
