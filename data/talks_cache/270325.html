STPJS 325

The disappointment of hopes and expectations at the resurrection would be indescribably dreadful.1

Angels and Spirits

The organization of the spiritual and heavenly worlds,2 and of spiritual and heavenly beings, was agreeable to the most perfect order and harmony:3 their limits and bounds were fixed4 irrevocably, and voluntarily subscribed to in their heavenly estate by themselves, and were by our first parents5 subscribed to upon the earth. Hence the importance of embracing and subscribing to principles of eternal truth by all men upon the earth that expect eternal life.6

I assure the Saints that truth, in reference to these matters, can and may be known through the revelations of God in the way of His ordinances,7 and in answer to prayer.8 The Hebrew Church “came unto the spirits of just men made perfect,9 and unto an innumerable company of angels,10 unto God the Father of all,11 and to Jesus Christ the Mediator of the new covenant.”12 What did they learn by coming to the spirits of just men made perfect?13 Is it written? No. What they learned has not been and could not have been written.14 What object was gained by this communication with the spirits of the just?15 It was the established order of the kingdom16 of God: The keys of power and knowledge17 were with them to communicate to the Saints. Hence the importance of understanding the distinction between the spirits of the just and angels.18

Spirits can only be revealed in flaming fire and glory.19 Angels have advanced further, their light and glory being tabernacled;20 and hence they appear in bodily shape.21 The spirits of just men22 are made ministering servants to those who23 are sealed unto life eternal,24 and it is through them that the sealing power comes down.

Patriarch Adams is now one of the spirits of the just men made perfect;25 and, if revealed now, must be revealed in fire;26 and the glory could not be endured.27 Jesus showed Himself to His disciples, and they thought it was His spirit, and they were afraid to approach His spirit.28 Angels have advanced higher in knowledge and power than spirits.
