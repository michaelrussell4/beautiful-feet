“Try the Spirits”17
The Prophet’s Editorial in the Times and Seasons

Recent occurrences that have transpired amongst us render it an imperative duty18 devolving upon me to say something in relation to the spirits by which men are actuated.

It is evident from the Apostles’ writings, that many false spirits existed in their day, and had “gone forth into the world,”19 and that it needed intelligence which God alone could impart to detect false spirits,20 and to prove what spirits were of God.21 The world in general have been grossly ignorant in regard to this one thing, and why should they be otherwise—for “the things of God knoweth no man, but the Spirit of God.”22

The Egyptians were not able to discover the difference between the miracles of Moses and those of the magicians until they came to be tested together;23 and if Moses had not appeared in their midst, they would unquestionably have thought that the miracles of the magicians were performed through the mighty power of God,24 for they were great miracles that were performed by them—a supernatural agency was developed, and great power manifested.
