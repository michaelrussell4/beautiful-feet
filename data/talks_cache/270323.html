The Mission of Elijah

Salvation3 could not come to the world without the mediation of Jesus Christ.4

How shall God come to the rescue5 of this generation? He will send Elijah the prophet.6 The law revealed to Moses in Horeb never was revealed to the children of Israel as a nation.7 Elijah shall reveal the covenants8 to seal the hearts of the fathers to the children, and the children to the fathers.9

The anointing and sealing is to be called, elected and made sure.10

“Without father, without mother, without descent, having neither beginning of days nor end of life, but made like unto the Son of God, abideth a priest continually.”11 The Melchizedek Priesthood holds the right from the eternal God,12 and not by descent from father and mother; and that priesthood is as eternal13 as God Himself,14 having neither beginning of days nor end of life.

The 2nd Priesthood is Patriarchal authority.15 Go to and finish the temple, and God will fill it with power,16 and you will then receive more knowledge concerning this priesthood.17

The 3rd is what is called the Levitical Priesthood, consisting of priests to administer in outward ordinances,18 made without an oath; but the Priesthood of Melchizedek is by an oath and covenant.19

The Holy Ghost is God’s messenger20 to administer in all those priesthoods.21

Jesus Christ is the heir of this Kingdom22—the Only Begotten of the Father23 according to the flesh,24 and holds the keys over all this world.25

Men have to suffer that they may come upon Mount Zion26 and be exalted above the heavens.27

I know a man that has been caught up to the third heavens,28 and can say, with Paul, that we have seen and heard things that are not lawful to utter.29 (Aug. 27, 1843.) DHC 5:554–556.
