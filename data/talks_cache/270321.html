STPJS 321
Covenants of the Fathers Revealed

Who is there that would not give all his goods to feed the poor,1 and pour out his gold and silver to the four winds,2 to go where Judge Higbee has gone?

That which hath been hid from before the foundation of the world3 is revealed to babes and sucklings4 in the last days.

The world is reserved unto burning5 in the last days. He shall send Elijah the prophet, and he shall reveal the covenants of the fathers in relation to the children, and the covenants of the children in relation to the fathers.6

Four destroying angels7 holding power over the four quarters of the earth8 until the servants of God9 are sealed in their foreheads,10 which signifies sealing the blessing11 upon their heads, meaning the everlasting covenant,12 thereby making their calling and election sure.13 When a seal is put upon the father and mother, it secures their posterity, so that they cannot be lost, but will be saved by virtue of the covenant of their father and mother.14

To the mourners I would say—Do as the husband and the father would instruct you, and you shall be reunited.

The speaker continued to teach the doctrine of election15 and the sealing powers16 and principles, and spoke of the doctrine of election with the seed of Abraham,17 and the sealing of blessings upon his posterity, and the sealing of the fathers and children,18 according to the declarations of the prophets. He then spoke of Judge Higbee in the world of spirits,19 and the blessings which he would obtain, and of the kind spirit and disposition of Judge Higbee while living; none of which was reported. (Aug. 13, 1843.) DHC 5:529–531.

The Priesthood

President Smith read the 7th Chap. Hebrews20 and said: Salem21 is designed for a Hebrew term. It should be Shiloam, which signifies righteousness and peace; as it is, it is nothing—neither Hebrew, Greek, Latin, French, nor any other language.

I say to all those who are disposed to set up stakes for the Almighty, You will come short of the glory of God.22

To become a joint heir23 of the heirship of the Son, one must put away24 all his false traditions.25
