When things that are of the greatest importance are passed over by the weak-minded men without even a thought, I want to see truth in all its bearings and hug it to my bosom. I believe all that God ever revealed,1 and I never hear of a man being damned for believing too much; but they are damned for unbelief.2

They found fault with Jesus Christ because He said He was the Son of God, and made Himself equal with God.3 They say of me, like they did of the Apostles of old, that I must be put down.4 What did Jesus say? “Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are Gods?5 If He called them Gods unto whom the word of God came, and the Scriptures cannot be broken, say ye of Him whom the Father hath sanctified and sent into the world, Thou blasphemest; because I said I am the Son of God?”6 It was through Him that they drank of the spiritual rock.7 Of course He would take the honor to Himself.8 Jesus, if they were called Gods unto whom the word of God came, why should it be thought blasphemy that I should say I am the Son of God?9

Eternal Glories

Go and read the vision in the Book of Covenants.10 There is clearly illustrated glory upon glory—one glory of the sun, another glory of the moon, and a glory of the stars;11 and as one star differeth from another star in glory,12 even so do they of the telestial world differ in glory,13 and every man who reigns in celestial glory is a God to his dominions.14 By the apostates admitting the testimony of the Doctrine and Covenants they damn themselves. Paul, what do you say? They impeached Paul15 and all went and left him. Paul had seven churches,16 and they drove him off from among them;17 and yet they cannot do it by me. I rejoice in that. My testimony is good.

Paul says, “There is one glory of the sun, and another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars; for one star differeth from another star in glory. So also is the resurrection of the dead.”18 They who obtain a glorious resurrection19 from the dead, are exalted far above principalities, powers, thrones, dominions and angels,20 and are expressly declared to be heirs of God and joint heirs with Jesus Christ,21 all having eternal power.22
