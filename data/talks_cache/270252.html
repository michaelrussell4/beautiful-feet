When Egypt was under the superintendence of Joseph2 it prospered, because he was taught of God;3 when they oppressed the Israelites,4 destruction came upon them.5 When the children of Israel were chosen with Moses at their head, they were to be a peculiar people,6 among whom God should place His name;7 their motto was: “The Lord is our lawgiver; the Lord is our Judge; the Lord is our King; and He shall reign over us.”8 While in this state they might truly say, “Happy is that people, whose God is the Lord.”9 Their government was a theocracy; they had God to make their laws,10 and men chosen by Him to administer them;11 He was their God, and they were His people.12 Moses received the word of the Lord from God Himself;13 he was the mouth of God to Aaron,14 and Aaron taught the people,15 in both civil and ecclesiastical affairs; they were both one, there was no distinction; so will it be when the purposes of God shall be accomplished:16 when “the Lord shall be King over the whole earth”17 and “Jerusalem His throne.”18 “The law shall go forth from Zion, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.”19

Universal Peace to Come from God

This is the only thing that can bring about the “restitution of all things spoken of by all the holy Prophets since the world was”20—“the dispensation of the fullness of times, when God shall gather together all things in one.”21 Other attempts to promote universal peace and happiness in the human family have proved abortive; every effort has failed; every plan and design has fallen to the ground; it needs the wisdom of God,22 the intelligence of God,23 and the power of God to accomplish this. The world has had a fair trial for six thousand years; the Lord will try the seventh thousand Himself;24 “He whose right it is,25 will possess the kingdom,26 and reign until He has put all things under His feet;”27 iniquity will hide its hoary head,28 Satan will be bound,29 and the works of darkness destroyed;30 righteousness will be put to the line, and judgment to the plummet,31 and “he that fears the Lord will alone be exalted in that day.”32 To bring about this state of things, there must of necessity be great confusion among the nations of the earth; “distress of nations with perplexity.”33 Am I asked what is the cause of
