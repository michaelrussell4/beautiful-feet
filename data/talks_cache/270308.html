God in any age of the world? I can never find much to say in expounding a text. A man never has half so much fuss to unlock a door, if he has a key, as though he had not, and had to cut it open with his jack-knife.

The main object was to build unto the Lord a house1 whereby He could reveal unto His people the ordinances of His house2 and the glories of His kingdom,3 and teach the people the way of salvation;4 for there are certain ordinances and principles that, when they are taught and practiced, must be done in a place or house built for that purpose.5

Principles of the Gospel Never Change

It was the design of the councils of heaven before the world was,6 that the principles and laws of the priesthood7 should be predicated upon the gathering of the people in every age of the world. Jesus did everything to gather the people, and they would not be gathered, and He therefore poured out curses upon them.8 Ordinances instituted in the heavens before the foundation of the world,9 in the priesthood, for the salvation of men, are not to be altered or changed.10 All must be saved on the same principles.11

Salvation for the Dead

It is for the same purpose that God gathers together His people in the last days,12 to build unto the Lord a house13 to prepare them for the ordinances and endowments, washings and anointings,14 etc. One of the ordinances of the house of the Lord is baptism for the dead.15 God decreed before the foundation of the world that that ordinance should be administered in a font prepared for that purpose in the house of the Lord.16 “This is only your opinion, sir,” says the sectarian. * * *

If a man gets a fullness of the priesthood of God17 he has to get it in the same way18 that Jesus Christ obtained it, and that was by keeping all the commandments19 and obeying all the ordinances of the house of the Lord.20

Where there is no change of priesthood, there is no change of ordinances, says Paul.21 If God has not changed the ordinances and the priesthood, howl, ye sectarians! If he has, when and where has He revealed it? Have ye turned revelators? Then why deny revelation?22
