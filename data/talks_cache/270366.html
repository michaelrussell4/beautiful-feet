I calculate to be one of the instruments of setting up the kingdom of Daniel3 by the word of the Lord,4 and I intend to lay a foundation5 that will revolutionize the whole world. I once offered my life6 to the Missouri mob as a sacrifice for my people, and here I am. It will not be by sword or gun that this kingdom will roll on: the power of truth is such that all nations will be under the necessity of obeying7 the Gospel. The prediction is that army will be against army:8 it may be that the Saints will have to beat their ploughs into swords,9 for it will not do for men to sit down patiently and see their children destroyed.10

The Resurrection

My text is on the resurrection of the dead, which you will find in the 14th chapter of John—“In my Father’s house are many mansions.”11 It should be—“In my Father’s kingdom are many kingdoms,” in order that ye may be heirs of God and joint-heirs with me.12 I do not believe the Methodist doctrine of sending honest men and noble-minded men to hell, along with the murderer13 and the adulterer.14 They may hurl all their hell and fiery billows15 upon me, for they will roll off me as fast as they come on. But I have an order of things16 to save the poor fellows at any rate, and get them saved; for I will send men to preach to them in prison17 and save them if I can.

Salvation for the Dead

There are mansions for those who obey a celestial law,18 and there are other mansions for those who come short19 of the law every man in his own order.20 There is baptism for those to exercise who are alive,21 and baptism for the dead22 who die without the knowledge of the Gospel.23

I am going on in my progress for eternal life.24 It is not only necessary that you should be baptized for your dead,25 but you will have to go through all the ordinances for them, the same as you have gone through to save yourselves.26 There will be 144,00027 saviors28 on Mount Zion,29 and with them an innumerable host that no man can number.30 Oh!
