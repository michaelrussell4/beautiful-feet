To go with that society who have not obeyed His commands.23

Baptism and the Gift of the Holy Ghost

Baptism is a sign to God, to angels, and to heaven that we do the will of God,24 and there is no other way25 beneath the heavens26 whereby God hath ordained for man to come to Him to be saved,27 and enter into the Kingdom of God, except faith in Jesus Christ, repentance, and baptism for the remission of sins, and any other course is in vain;28 then you have the promise of the gift of the Holy Ghost.29
