The world always mistook false prophets1 for true ones, and those that were sent of God, they considered to be false prophets and hence they killed, stoned, punished and imprisoned the true prophets, and these had to hide themselves “in deserts and dens, and caves of the earth,”2 and though the most honorable men of the earth,3 they banished them from their society as vagabonds, whilst they cherished, honored and supported knaves, vagabonds, hypocrites, impostors, and the basest of men.4

The Gift of Discernment of Spirits

A man must have the discerning of spirits,5 as we before stated, to understand these things, and how is he to obtain this gift if there are no gifts of the Spirit? And how can these gifts be obtained without revelation?6 “Christ ascended into heaven, and gave gifts to men;7 and He gave some Apostles, and some Prophets, and some Evangelists, and some Pastors and Teachers.”8 And how were Apostles, Prophets, Pastors, Teachers and Evangelists chosen?9 By prophecy (revelation) and by laying on of hands10—by a divine communication, and a divinely appointed ordinance—through the medium of the Priesthood, organized according to the order of God,11 by divine appointment.12 The Apostles in ancient times held the keys of this Priesthood13—of the mysteries of the Kingdom14 of God, and consequently were enabled to unlock and unravel all things pertaining to the government of the Church,15 the welfare of society, the future destiny of men, and the agency, power and influence of spirits; for they could control them at pleasure, bid them depart in the name of Jesus,16 and detect their mischievous and mysterious operations when trying to palm themselves upon the Church in a religious garb, and militate against the interest of the Church and spread of truth. We read that they “cast out devils in the name of Jesus,”17 and when a woman possessing the spirit of divination, cried before Paul and Silas, “these are the servants of the Most High God that show unto us the way of salvation,”18 they detected the spirit.
