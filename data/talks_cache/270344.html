If any man does not know God,2 and inquires what kind of a being he is,—if he will search diligently his own heart3—if the declaration of Jesus and the apostles be true, he will realize that he has not eternal life;4 for there can be eternal life on no other principle.

My first object is to find out the character of the only wise and true God,5 and what kind of a being he is; and if I am so fortunate as to be the man to comprehend God,6 and explain or convey the principles to your hearts, so that the Spirit seals7 them upon you, then let every man and woman henceforth sit in silence,8 put their hands on their mouths,9 and never lift their hands10 or voices,11 or say anything against the man of God12 or the servants of God13 again. But if I fail to do it, it becomes my duty to renounce all further pretensions to revelations and inspirations, or to be a prophet; and I should be like the rest of the world—a false teacher,14 be hailed as a friend, and no man would seek my life.15 But if all religious teachers were honest enough to renounce their pretensions to godliness16 when their ignorance of the knowledge of God is made manifest,17 they will all be as badly off as I am, at any rate; and you might as well take the lives of other false teachers as that of mine, if I am false. If any man is authorized to take away my life because he thinks and says I am a false teacher, then, upon the same principle, we should be justified in taking away the life of every false teacher, and where would be the end of blood? And who would not be the sufferer?a

The Privilege of Religious Freedom

But meddle not18 with any man for his religion:19 and all governments ought to permit every man to enjoy his religion unmolested.20 No man is authorized to take away life in consequence of difference of religion, which all laws and governments ought to tolerate and protect, right or wrong.
