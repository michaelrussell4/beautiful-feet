Calling and Election

Now, there is some grand secret6 here, and keys to unlock the subject. Notwithstanding the apostle exhorts them to add to their faith, virtue, knowledge, temperance,7 etc., yet he exhorts them to make their calling and election sure.8 And though they had heard an audible voice from heaven bearing testimony that Jesus was the Son of God,9 yet he says we have a more sure word of prophecy, whereunto ye do well that ye take heed as unto a light shining in a dark place.10 Now, wherein could they have a more sure word of prophecy than to hear the voice of God saying, This is my beloved Son.11

Now for the secret and grand key. Though they might hear the voice of God and know that Jesus was the Son of God, this would be no evidence that their election and calling was made sure, that they had part with Christ,12 and were joint heirs with Him.13 They then would want that more sure word of prophecy,14 that they were sealed in the heavens15 and had the promise of eternal life in the kingdom of God.16 Then, having this promise sealed17 unto them, it was an anchor to the soul, sure and steadfast.18 Though the thunders might roll and lightnings flash, and earthquakes bellow, and war gather thick around, yet this hope and knowledge would support the soul in every hour of trial, trouble and tribulation.19 Then knowledge through our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ20 is the grand key that unlocks the glories and mysteries of the kingdom21 of heaven.

Compare this principle once with Christendom at the present day, and where are they, with all their boasted religion, piety and sacredness while at the same time they are crying out against prophets, apostles, angels, revelations, prophesying and visions, etc. Why, they are just ripening for the damnation22 of hell.23 They will be damned, for they reject the most glorious principles of the Gospel of Jesus Christ24 and treat with disdain
