He then commenced reading the 13th chapter—“Though I speak with the tongues of men and angels, and have not charity, I am become as sounding brass, or a tinkling cymbal;”1 and said, don’t be limited in your views with regard to your neighbor’s virtue, but beware of self-righteousness, and be limited in the estimate of your own virtues,2 and not think yourselves more righteous than others;3 you must enlarge your souls4 towards each other, if you would do like Jesus, and carry your fellow-creatures to Abraham’s bosom.5 He said he had manifested long-suffering,6 forbearance and patience towards the Church, and also to his enemies;7 and we must bear with each other’s failings,8 as an indulgent parent bears9 with the foibles of his children.10

President Smith then read the 2nd verse—“Though I have the gift of prophecy, and understand all mysteries, and all knowledge; and though I have all faith, so that I could remove mountains, and have not charity, I am nothing.”11 He then said, though a man should become mighty, do great things, overturn mountains, perform mighty works,12 and should then turn from his high station to do evil, to eat and drink with the drunken, all his former deeds would not save him, but he would go to destruction!13 As you increase in innocence and virtue, as you increase in goodness, let your hearts expand, let them be enlarged towards others; you must be long-suffering, and bear with the faults and errors of mankind.

How precious are the souls of men!14 The female part of the community are apt to be contracted in their views. You must not be contracted, but you must be liberal in your feelings. Let this Society teach women how to behave towards their husbands,15 to treat them with mildness and affection. When a man is borne down with trouble, when he is perplexed with care and difficulty, if he can meet a smile instead of an argument or a murmur16—if he can meet with mildness, it will calm down his soul and soothe his feelings; when the mind is going to despair, it needs a solace17 of affection and kindness.
