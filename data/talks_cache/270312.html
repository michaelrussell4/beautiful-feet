STPJS 312

Peter1 and Stephen testify2 that they saw the Son of Man standing on the right hand of God.3 Any person that had seen the heavens opened4 knows that there are three personages in the heavens who hold the keys of power, and one presides over all.

If any man attempts to refute5 what I am about to say, after I have made it plain, let him beware.6

The Son Does What the Father Did

As the Father hath power in Himself, so hath the Son power in Himself,7 to lay down His life and take it again,8 so He has a body of His own. The Son doeth what He hath seen the Father do:9 then the Father hath some day laid down His life and taken it again;10 so He has a body of His own;11 each one will be in His own body; and yet the sectarian world believe the body of the Son is identical with the Father’s.

Gods have an ascendency over the angels, who are ministering servants.12 In the resurrection, some are raised to be angels, others are raised to become Gods.13

These things are revealed in the most holy places in a Temple14 prepared for that purpose. Many of the sects cry out, “Oh, I have the testimony of Jesus; I have the spirit of God; but away with Joe Smith; he says he is a prophet; but there are to be no prophets or revelators in the last days.” Stop, sir! The Revelator says that the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy;15 so by your own mouth you are condemned.16 But to the text. Why gather the people together in this place? For the same purpose that Jesus wanted to gather the Jews17—to receive the ordinances, the blessings, and glories that God has in store for His Saints.18

I will now ask this assembly and all the Saints if you will now build this house19 and receive the ordinances and blessings which God has in store for you;20 or will you not build unto the Lord this house, and let Him pass by and bestow these blessings21 upon another people?22 I pause for a reply. (June 11, 1843.) DHC 5:423–427.
