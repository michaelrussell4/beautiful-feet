Again he says, “Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God;”8 and, “heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away.”9 If a man is born of water and of the Spirit, he can get into the kingdom of God. It is evident the kingdom of God was on the earth, and John prepared subjects for the kingdom,10 by preaching the Gospel to them and baptizing them,11 and he prepared the way before the Savior,12 or came as a forerunner, and prepared subjects for the preaching of Christ;13 and Christ preached through Jerusalem14 on the same ground where John had preached; and when the apostles were raised up, they worked in Jerusalem,15 and Jesus commanded them to tarry there until they were endowed16 with power from on high.17 Had they not work to do in Jerusalem? They did work, and prepared a people for the Pentecost.18 The kingdom of God was with them before the day of Pentecost, as well as afterwards; and it was also with John, and he preached the same Gospel and baptism that Jesus and the apostles preached after him.19 The endowment was to prepare the disciples for their missions unto the world.20

Divine Authority Necessary to Make Ordinances Valid

Whenever men can find out the will of God and find an administrator legally authorized from God, there is the kingdom of God; but where these are not, the kingdom of God is not. All the ordinances, systems, and administrations on the earth are of no use to the children of men,21 unless they are ordained and authorized of God;22 for nothing will save a man but a legal administrator; for none others will be acknowledged either by God or angels.23

I know what I say; I understand my mission and business.24 God Almighty is my shield;25 and what can man do if God is my friend?26 I shall not be sacrificed until my time comes;27 then I shall be offered freely.28 All flesh is as grass,29 and a governor
