Earth Now Groaning Under Corruption

The earth is groaning under corruption,2 oppression, tyranny and bloodshed; and God is coming out of His hiding place,3 as He said He would do, to vex the nations of the earth.4 Daniel, in his vision, saw convulsion upon convulsion; he “beheld till the thrones were cast down, and the Ancient of Days did sit;”5 and one was brought before him like unto the Son of Man; and all nations, kindred, tongues, and peoples, did serve and obey Him.6 It is for us to be righteous,7 that we may be wise and understand; for none of the wicked shall understand;8 but the wise shall understand,9 and they that turn many to righteousness shall shine as the stars for ever and ever.10

It Behooves Us to Be Wise

As a Church and a people it behooves us to be wise,11 and to seek to know the will of God,12 and then be willing to do it;13 for “blessed is he that heareth the word of the Lord, and keepeth it,”14 say the Scriptures. “Watch and pray always,” says our Savior, “that ye may be accounted worthy to escape the things that are to come on the earth, and to stand before the Son of Man.”15 If Enoch, Abraham, Moses, and the children of Israel, and all God’s people were saved by keeping the commandments of God,16 we, if saved at all, shall be saved upon the same principle. As God governed Abraham, Isaac and Jacob as families,17 and the children of Israel as a nation;18 so we, as a Church, must be under His guidance if we are prospered, preserved19 and sustained. Our only confidence can be in God;20 our only wisdom obtained from Him;21 and He alone must be our protector22 and safeguard,23 spiritually and temporally, or we fall.
