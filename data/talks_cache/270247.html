The Necessity of Prayer

The Lord cannot always be known by the thunder of His voice,8 by the display of His glory or by the manifestation of His power, and those that are the most anxious to see these things, are the least prepared to meet them,9 and were the Lord to manifest His powers as He did to the children of Israel,10 such characters would be the first to say, “Let not the Lord speak any more, lest we His people die.”11

We would say to the brethren, seek to know God in your closets,12 call upon him in the fields.13 Follow the directions of the Book of Mormon, and pray over, and for your families, your cattle, your flocks, your herds, your corn, and all things that you possess; ask the blessing of God upon all your labors, and everything that you engage in.14 Be virtuous and pure;15 be men of integrity and truth;16 keep the commandments of God;17 and then you will be able more perfectly to understand the difference between right and wrong—between the things of God and the things of men;18 and your path will be like that of the just, which shineth brighter and brighter unto the perfect day.19
